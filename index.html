The Eagles way to race.

We are the inline speed skating branch of Eagles Roller Sports Club. Our focus is simple: a welcoming team environment, quality coaching and the thrill of getting faster — whatever your starting line.

  • Inline speed skating — not ice speed skating
  • Development for new and progressing racers
  • Club culture built on teamwork, respect, discipline and fun

First visit

Come and try inline speed skating.

New to the sport? We welcome first-timers to a come-and-try session. Before you hit the track, you need two things sorted — and if you're not a member yet, there's an easy way to start.

  • Bring your own helmet — it's required to skate
  • Be a Skate Australia member

Not a member yet?

Skate Australia offers a free three-week trial so you can hit the track before you commit.
